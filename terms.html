This is not medical advice

Atrest is not a medical device and it is not treatment. It does not diagnose anything, it does not know anything about your health, and nothing in it should be taken as advice from a clinician. What it offers is somewhere to put a craving and a record of your own days, kept by you.

Nicotine dependence is a medical matter and there is real help for it. A doctor or pharmacist can talk to you about what is available. In Belgium, Tabacstop is free, anonymous and staffed by specialists on 0800 111 00. Elsewhere, your national quit line will do the same. If you are in danger or in a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.

Please do not stop, start or change any medication because of something in this app.

Age

The App Store rates Atrest 18+.

That rating is about subject matter. Apple's questionnaire asks how often an app refers to tobacco, alcohol or drug use, and for an app about quitting vaping the honest answer is "frequently" — which produces an 18+ rating whatever the reason for the references. The established quit-smoking apps carry the same rating for the same reason.

Atrest does not show vaping or smoking, does not depict any nicotine product, and does not encourage anyone to use one. It exists to help people stop. I have not sought a lower rating than the questionnaire produced.
