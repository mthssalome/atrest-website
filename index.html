The ritual

A craving lasts a few minutes. This is something to do with them.

Three minutes

Tap once. A tree grows on its own. There is no input to give, nothing to get right, and no way to do it badly.

A different tree every time

Each one grows from its own seed. Leave at twenty seconds and you keep what grew — an unfinished tree is kept exactly like a finished one.

Yours afterwards

The tree stays. Keep it or don't, share it or don't. Nothing asks you to.

What's in it

Five things, and none of them keeps score.

  • The craving ritual The largest thing on the screen, always one tap away.
  • A count of days since you started It doesn't reset, and logging a slip doesn't take it away.
  • A calendar you can mark honestly Logging a slip costs nothing. Un-logging it leaves no trace.
  • A line about who you're becoming In your own words, changeable the moment it stops being true.
  • Notes, typed or spoken Nothing reads them but you. Not the app, not me, not anything.

What isn't in it

  • No scores.
  • No badges.
  • No leaderboard.

Nothing that turns a bad day into a number you have to look at afterwards. A slip is a day you marked, and that is all it is.

Privacy

The app makes no network requests. Not one.

No account, no sign-in, no sync, no analytics, no third-party code of any kind. Everything you write stays in the app's own storage on your phone. Voice notes are written out by your own phone and are never sent anywhere.

This page loads nothing from anywhere else either.

Questions

What happens if I slip?

You mark the day, if you want to. The count of days since you started doesn't move, nothing is taken away, and nothing you wrote about yourself changes. Logging is never required — the app never assumes anything about a day you didn't mark.

Is it really free?

This version is entirely free. There is nothing to buy, no subscription, and no part of it held back. If a future version charges for something, it will be visible before you get there, and it won't take away anything you already have.

Does it work without a connection?

It only works without one, in the sense that it never uses one. There is no server, no account and no sync. Everything happens on the device.

Is this for vaping or for smoking?

It's built for vaping. Nothing in it is specific to one or the other — a craving is a craving — but the language and the design are aimed at people putting down a vape.

Why is it rated 18+?

Apple's age questionnaire asks how often an app refers to tobacco or drug use, and for an app about quitting vaping the honest answer is "frequently" — which produces an 18+ rating whatever the reason for the references. The established quit-smoking apps carry the same rating for the same reason. Atrest shows no nicotine product and encourages no one to use one.

Is this medical treatment?

No. Atrest is not a medical device, not treatment, and not advice from a clinician. Nicotine dependence is a medical matter and there is real help for it — a doctor or pharmacist can point you to it. In Belgium, Tabacstop is free and anonymous on 0800 111 00.
